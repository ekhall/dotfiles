Three buffers: hello → search (thread list) → show (one thread)

Compose & send

new message (from hello / search)

Fastmail sent mail is Fcc'd to fastmail/Sent and uploaded on the next G. Yale (Office 365) auto-saves to its Exchange Sent Items server-side, so G just pulls that copy down.
Two identities: msmtp picks the account from your From header — replies as kevin.hall@yale.edu go via Office 365 (XOAUTH2), everything else via Fastmail.
Search syntax (for s / j)
  • tag:inbox · tag:unread · tag:sent · tag:fastmail · tag:yale
  • from:laura · to:hall@absinthe.org · subject:invoice
  • date:today · date:7d.. (last 7 days)
  • date:2026-07-01..2026-07-13
  • combine with and / or / not:
    tag:inbox and from:tastytrade
My setup at a glance
  • Newest first is my default; o flips one buffer. Inside a thread, messages always read oldest→newest.
  • Three Sent folders (Sent, Sent Items, Sent Messages) all show under tag:sent.
  • Junk Mail → tag:spam, Trash → tag:deleted — both hidden from default searches.
  • Two accounts, one index: tag:fastmail / tag:yale filter by account; j i = both, j Y = Yale only.
  • Auto-sync every 5 min while Emacs is open; G forces it now (in the background — the UI no longer blocks).
  • d = delete moves mail to Fastmail Trash (recoverable ~30 days). Undo with -deleted before the next sync.
